# GeoDev-Lab-Week-1-Assessment
PROJECT BRIEF: 
Project Title: 
Assessing Health Facility Provision and Population Coverage in Kogi State Using GIS.
Research Question:
Are health facilities adequately distributed to serve the population across the Local Government Areas of Kogi State?
Why It Matters?
Access to healthcare depends on having enough health facilities to serve the population. This project will assess the number, type, and distribution of health facilities across the 21 LGAs of Kogi State and identify LGAs where the available facilities may be insufficient for the population.
Study Area:
Kogi State, Nigeria
Data Needed:
1. Kogi State boundary
2. Kogi State LGA boundaries
3. Health facility locations and types
4. Population data for each LGA
5. Recommended population-to-health-facility standards
Expected Output
A GIS analysis and maps showing the distribution of health facilities across Kogi State, the population served by each LGA's facilities, and areas with potential health facility deficits.
